## 🔍 2. Root Cause Analysis: The "ITR-1 vs. ITR-2 & Regime" Issue

### Why did the CPC issue the ₹11.93 Lakh disallowance notice?
* **Form Selection Error**: Last year, your return was submitted in **Form ITR-1 (Sahaj)** on **September 8, 2025**. 
* **The Disallowance**: The CPC's automated system processed the return under the **New Tax Regime** (where HRA and LTA are not allowed by law) and flagged the following claimed exemptions:
  * **HRA Exemption (u/s 10(13A))**: ₹3,79,471 (Disallowed ❌)
  * **LTA Exemption (u/s 10(5))**: ₹5,30,809 (Disallowed ❌)
  * **Meal & Special Allowances**: ₹2,83,506 (Disallowed ❌)
  * **Total Disallowed Exemption**: **₹11,93,786**

### Why is this disallowance legally incorrect?
1. **Timely Filing**: Your return was filed on **September 8, 2025**, on or before the applicable due date (September 30, 2025 u/s 139(1)).
2. **Valid Opt-Out**: You specifically selected **"Yes"** to opt out of the New Tax Regime u/s 115BAC(6).
3. **Form 67 Submitted**: You had already submitted **Form 67** on the portal declaring **$11.34 (₹960)** of gross US dividends and claiming **$2.75 (₹233)** of Foreign Tax Credit u/s 90. Because you held foreign US stocks, you were legally required to file **ITR-2** (with Schedule FA and Schedule CG) instead of ITR-1.
